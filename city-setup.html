This tool prepares a real-world-scale road and building blockout across Nashik's urban area. It uses OpenStreetMap instead of stretching the old College Road image. Your existing College Road world remains safe.

1. City road network

Real OSM coordinates, automatically converted to the game’s common metre scale.

2. Building footprints

Simple blocks wherever OpenStreetMap has building geometry. Detailed façades come later.

3. Nearby minimap

Follows the player and shows only nearby roads. Press M to view the full city.

Install the Nashik city map on your Mac

  1. Extract the v2.0 ZIP and open the extracted Nashik-Drive-main folder.
  2. Double-click BUILD_FULL_NASHIK_CITY.command (right-click → Open if macOS warns you).
  3. The tool downloads mapped roads and buildings in resumable chunks and creates city-world.json. Keep Terminal open as it runs.
  4. After the generator finishes, the game opens. Drive from College Road toward other areas of Nashik. Use M for the full city map.

Offline/manual option: If a map download fails, rerun the command to resume. You can also run python3 scripts/build_nashik_city.py --input /path/to/nashik.geojson with a compatible OSM GeoJSON or Overpass JSON file. The supplied city-world.json is included in the v3.0 project.
